BitLut is a production Android bridge from Huawei Health to Android Health Connect.

Package:
com.openhealth.sync

Requested Huawei Health Kit scopes:
- HEALTHKIT_STEP_READ
- HEALTHKIT_DISTANCE_READ
- HEALTHKIT_ACTIVITY_READ
- HEALTHKIT_ACTIVITY_RECORD_READ
- HEALTHKIT_HISTORYDATA_OPEN_WEEK

Why these scopes are required:
- Step read: imports daily step records from Huawei Health.
- Distance read: imports walking/running distance when available.
- Activity read: imports real user activity signals when available.
- Activity record read: imports real exercise/activity records when available.
- Historical data open week: allows recent historical sync after installation or missed background sync.

Data policy:
- BitLut reads only real user data returned by Huawei Health Kit.
- BitLut writes available records to Android Health Connect.
- BitLut does not generate mock health data.
- BitLut does not create fake steps, fake workouts, fake calories, or placeholder records.
- Empty Huawei responses are treated as no data, not fabricated data.

Reviewer test flow:
1. Install the signed release APK.
2. Install/update HMS Core.
3. Install/update Huawei Health.
4. Sign in to Huawei Health.
5. Make sure Huawei Health contains real steps/activity data.
6. Open BitLut.
7. Grant Android Health Connect permissions.
8. Tap Huawei Health.
9. Approve Huawei Health Kit permissions.
10. Tap Sync.
11. Verify records appear in Android Health Connect.

Known blocker before verification:
If Huawei Health Kit returns 50005, this means the requested Health Kit scopes are not yet approved server-side for this app/package/release SHA-256/scope set. User permission inside Huawei Health alone is not enough.
